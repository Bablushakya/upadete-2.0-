01 / Introduction

Are you source from India, but don't know where to begin ?

We can help you.

We are offering a guided sourcing trip to India — designed for business owners, makers, and product developers who are ready to source with confidence. Spots are limited to 8–12 participants per trip, allowing as much individual guidance as needed.

Why This Trip?

01 . More Than A Sourcing Trip

Business & Cultural Immersion

We combine our sourcing journey with a beautiful cultural experience. You'll explore vibrant local markets, discover breathtaking places, and connect with India far beyond the Trade show floor.

02 . Explore India

Where Business Meets Beauty

India offers unparalleled design heritage, artisan craftsmanship, and a richness of culture that will inspire your creative vision. Every day will hold a new possibility.

03 . Kim Guides Every Step

Never Navigate Alone

I have made this journey many times on my own. I handle the translator, the factory introductions, the cultural nuances — so you can focus entirely on building your business.

Following the journey through India

7-Day Sourcing Itinerary

Day 01 October 12th, 2026

Arrival & Welcome

Airport pickup and transfer to boutique lodging followed by an evening welcome dinner and sourcing orientation.

Day 02 October 13th, 2026

Discover Old Delhi & Acclimate

A gentle day of rest, exploring historical Old Delhi markets, tasting local spices, and acclimating to the rich local environment.

Day 03 October 14th, 2026

IHGF Delhi Trade Show

Explore the premier IHGF Delhi Fair, one of Asia's largest gift and home decor trade shows, to identify potential manufacturing and artisan partners.

Day 04 October 15th, 2026

IHGF Sourcing Depth

A second dedicated day at the trade show for deep-dive supplier meetings, hands-on product evaluations, and custom sampling arrangements.

Day 05 October 16th, 2026

Jaipur Factory Floor Visits

Travel to Jaipur for direct visits with vetted manufacturers, touring facility floors and meeting development and QA teams.

Day 06 October 17th, 2026

Jaipur Artisan Discovery

Explore Jaipur's artisan communities, block printing workshops, local fabric craft markets, and historical heritage sites.

Day 07 October 18th, 2026

Wrap-Up & Departure

Savor a scenic private car journey from Jaipur back to Delhi, with a personal driver. We will make stops to admire the stunning landscapes, culminating at the airport for your departure. It'll be a perfect farewell to your journey!

Logistics, Inclusions & Pricing

What's Included

  • Airport pickup, transfer & private ground transportation throughout India
  • Premium heritage hotel & boutique lodging accommodations
  • Professionally guided factory floor tours & direct artisan introductions
  • English-Hindi translation assistance and cultural bridge support
  • Pre-trip sourcing consultations, luggage planning, and travel orientations

Investment Details

Consultation-Based Pricing

  • • Discovery Call : Pricing details are shared during your introductory discovery call to fit your business goals.
  • • Qualified Applicants: Receive full trip pricing and payment schedules immediately after application approval.
  • • Deposit Process: A placement deposit is required to secure your spot after acceptance.
  • • Payment Schedule: Installment payment terms are available for early registrants.
  • • Cancellation Policy: Details regarding flexible cancellation and refund terms will be provided in your custom trip contract.
*Flexible business financing options may be discussed during your discovery call.

What's Not Included

  • International airfare to and from New Delhi
  • international travel & medical insurance
  • Passport fees & Indian visa application costs
  • Most daily lunch & dinner meals
