Usage Notes
  • Body background: warm cream + soft peach/blush radial gradients.
  • Cards/panels: ivory with gold semi-transparent borders.
  • Buttons: terracotta main color with gold accents and warm shadows.
  • Text: dark brown/charcoal for readability and warm muted tones for supporting content.
